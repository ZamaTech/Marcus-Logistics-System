ABOUT THE COMPANY

About Us

Marcus Logistics and Shuttle Services (Pty) Ltd

Marcus Logistics and Shuttle Services (Pty) Ltd is a Polokwane-based transport and delivery company focused on dependable movement for people, parcels, and businesses.

We combine professional service, careful planning, and well-managed journeys to deliver safe, comfortable, and punctual transport across local and long-distance routes nationwide throughout South Africa.

THE FOUNDER

Built with purpose. Operated with care.

Marcus Tiisetso Leso · Founder and Director

Marcus Tiisetso Leso founded Marcus Logistics and Shuttle Services at the age of 23, establishing the company in Polokwane with a clear commitment to safe, comfortable, and dependable transport.

His vision continues to guide the company’s approach: respect every passenger, handle every delivery carefully, and make punctual service part of the standard.
